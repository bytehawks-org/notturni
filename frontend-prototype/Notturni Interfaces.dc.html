Why write slowly

There is a kind of writing that happens only after the house has gone quiet. It is not faster or better; it is simply less observed. The margin is where a text admits it is unfinished, and a footnote is the most honest sentence a writer can offer.1

Save fragmentCopy linkQuote31 words · 4% of post

Medium taught us to clap. Substack taught us to subscribe. Almost nobody taught us to keep what we read. A fragment is a reader's own underline: a selection saved to a private shelf, re-highlighted every time the page is opened again, from any door.2

“Nessuna intelligenza artificiale, solo una tastiera che separa i pensieri da un'interfaccia pulita.”

The margin as a room

Pasting a bare link produces a quiet card rather than an unfurled banner. The author decides; the reader is not ambushed.3 Mentions like @marta resolve by id, so a renamed user never breaks a sentence.
